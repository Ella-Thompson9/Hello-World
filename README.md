### University Ratings Analysis
For this project, my group analyzed data from the 2025 Times Higher Education World University Rankings to see what factors are related to a university’s overall rating. We used a random sample of 200 universities and looked at factors such as teaching, research, international engagement, and student-to-faculty ratios. Using multiple regression analysis, we explored how these factors relate to overall university ratings and whether there are differences between universities in different regions.

## Description

This project focuses on analyzing university ratings using data from the 2025 Times Higher Education World University Rankings. Our goal was to understand which factors, such as teaching, research, citations, international engagement, and student-to-faculty ratios, are most closely related to a university’s overall rating. We selected a random sample of 200 universities from the original dataset of 1,904 universities and used multiple regression analysis to examine relationships between these variables. We also considered differences between geographic regions and other university characteristics to identify patterns in university ratings. This project helped us explore how data analytics can be used to better understand university performance and compare institutions around the world.

## Tools Used

Microsoft Excel: Used to organize and randomly sample the university ratings dataset
Kaggle: Source of the original university ratings dataset from the 2025 Times Higher Education World University Rankings
